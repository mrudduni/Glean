# Field Shift: pitch

Project: **Field Shift** (NASA Space Apps 2026, "Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data"). Team Brackets.

Check the 2026 rules for the required format. Past years allowed a 30-second video or up to 7 slides. Do not use NASA logos or the insignia in any frame.

## 30-second video script

Record from the live site at 1080p. Burn in captions. No music needed. Say numbers only after the final data is in the app, then fill each `[TODO]` from the screen, not from memory.

## Slides (if slides are allowed, up to 7)

1. **The problem.** Ogallala decline, hot summers at corn pollination, sandy soils with a Dust Bowl history. Add one map. Source each fact.
2. **Field Shift.** One sentence on what it does and who it is for. Screenshot of the strategy cards.
3. **How it decides.** Data to model to cards. Name the NASA data: POWER, MODIS, SMAP, IMERG, GRACE, ECOSTRESS, SRTM, plus HLS and OpenET if used. Say what each one does in a few words.
4. **Rotation Replay.** Drought-year chart with the 2012 band. State the water difference from the app.
5. **Farms Like Yours and the assistant.** Neighbour mini-map, table with n, number-check badge. Say it is observational.
6. **Honesty.** Validation badges (satellite versus ground), the gaps list on each card, the skipped datasets and why.
7. **Impact and next steps.** Water in mm and inches per field, Spanish support, extension-agent handoff, going beyond Kansas with global data such as ERA5 and GLDAS.

## Three lines to say if you get only ten seconds

- Field Shift compares your rotation with three others on 25 years of real weather and shows why each one was suggested.
- Every number comes from NASA data or a named source, and the assistant flags any number it cannot trace.
- It supports your judgement and your extension agent. It does not replace them.

## Answers to likely judge questions

- **Is this a prediction?** No. It replays past weather and compares options. We say so on every card.
- **How do you know the water model is sound?** It uses the FAO-56 method and is checked against OpenET and SMAP. The trust page shows each check and how well it matched.
- **What about costs?** Income is shown before costs. This is listed as a gap on each card.
- **Why Spanish?** Many farm workers and farmers in the region are more at ease in Spanish. The whole app, glossary and assistant work in both.
- **Does the AI make things up?** The assistant only sees the field's computed facts. A number check marks each number as verified or unverified.
