## Common themes so far

- The most common pitch is "AI recommends a rotation from NASA weather, soil and crop data".
- Two teams stress explainability: Rotavise ("clear reasons for each recommendation") and AgroDecision ("transparent recommendations").
- AgroDecision plans a what-if simulator and a water-need comparison. This is the closest stated match to a farmer-priority trade-off tool.
- Few descriptions name specific datasets. FieldSignals names heat, rainfall and vegetation data only.
- No team I read mentions market or price data, GAEZ suitability, Sentinel-2 or soil-health outcomes such as organic matter or erosion. These could set our tool apart, but this is not confirmed beyond the 13 teams read.
