# 2026 space agency partners

Space Apps 2026 lists 17 space agency partners. Using partner data is encouraged but not required. A Global Award needs at least one NASA data source or resource. Executive judges for the 2026 Global Winners come from NASA and these partners.

## Partners and agriculture relevance

## What this means for the rotation tool

- ESA data is the only partner data the Field Shift challenge itself points to. Sentinel-2 L2A and ERA5 or ERA5-Land are documented in `../01_datasets/global/sentinel2.md` and `../01_datasets/global/era5.md`. Using one of them shows partner-data use to judges, who include partner agency executives at the final stage.
- The challenge also lists HLS through Landsat. HLS blends ESA Sentinel-2 with NASA Landsat, so it counts as NASA data built partly on partner data.
- Other partners have well-known farm-relevant missions, for example radar from CONAE, ISRO and CSA, and JAXA land and rainfall products. None of these are on the partners page or the Field Shift Resources tab, so their data, access and licences are not confirmed here and would need separate checks.
- The 2026 Space Apps challenge list also includes "Dancing with the SARs", which uses NASA-ISRO NISAR radar to track surface change including farming activity. This may be a NASA plus ISRO data route for field history, but it is not researched here.
